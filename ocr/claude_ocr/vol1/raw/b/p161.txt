---NOTES---
- In the heading of entry ١٤٩٦ the book prints «بنُ مُرَادِ» with a ḍamma, where «بنِ» would be expected. I kept it as printed.
- The heading of entry ١٤٩٧ is vowelled «الأَبْيَارِيُّ», but its first teacher is spelled «الإبياري». Both are kept as printed.
- «أحمدبن» and «أحمدالخياري» are printed without a space, and I kept them that way.
