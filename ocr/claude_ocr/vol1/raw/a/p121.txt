---NOTES---
- Every slice has a vertical line down the middle that goes through the text. It looks like a scanning artifact, and I left it out.
- The last lines are printed in two columns. I kept each pair on one line, with the right column first and a tab before the left column.
- «لأنَّ في السلسلة عدد» is copied exactly as printed. Standard grammar would expect «عددًا» here.
- The vertical line crosses «القُرَشِيُّ», «قُوَيْدَر» and «صَمَادِيَّة», so the diacritics on those three words are hard to read with certainty.
