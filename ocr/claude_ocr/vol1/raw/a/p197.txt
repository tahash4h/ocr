---NOTES---
- «حتى الآن» .: this line has a closing » after الآن, but there is no opening quote for it. I kept it as printed.
- «الشّهْدَاوِيُّ»: the shadda looks like it sits on the ش with no vowel mark. This is my best reading of the marks.
- «محمت»: printed this way. It is probably a local spelling of «محمد», so I did not change it.
