---NOTES---
- Each two-column row has the right column first, then a tab, then the left column.
- "عبداالسلام" (١٤٢٣) has a doubled alef in print. I kept it as printed.
- "الهوراي" (١٥٠٠) may be a printing error for الهواري. I kept it as printed.
- "إبراهيم" (١٥٦٤) is hard to make out and may be printed as "إبراهيم" without the ب. My best reading is إبراهيم [؟].
- ١٥٤٨ and ١٥٥٠ have no dash after the number, as printed.
- The birth date "١٣٠٣ـ١٣٠٥" is printed as a range.
